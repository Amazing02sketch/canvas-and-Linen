Frequently Asked Questions

What if I lose my file or didn't receive the email?

Check your Promotions/Spam folder first, or email us at hello@canvasandlinenstudio.com with your order receipt and we will re-send your download link right away.

Are physical products shipped to my address?

No—all items in Canvas & Linen Studio are 100% digital downloads. That means zero shipping fees, zero wait time, and instant access worldwide.

What is your refund policy on digital downloads?

Because digital files cannot be returned once downloaded, sales are generally final. However, if a file is corrupted or you accidentally bought a duplicate, we will gladly fix or refund it.

Who runs Canvas & Linen Studio?

We are an independent educational design studio creating calm, research-backed planners and Montessori printables. Connect with us anytime on our official Facebook Page or email.
